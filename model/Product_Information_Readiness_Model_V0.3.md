# Product Information Readiness Model V0.3

## Core Goal

**Evaluate the AI-readiness of a product listing.**  
评估一份商品信息是否具备 AI 可读与可推荐的准备度。

Current project focus:

> **How can product information increase the chance of being selected by AI?**  
> 商品信息如何提高被 AI 选中的概率？

## Five Dimensions

### 1. Accessibility｜信息可达性
Can AI access and read the available product information?

### 2. Information Quality｜信息质量
Is the information accurate, fresh, consistent, sufficiently complete, and free of harmful conflicts?

Core aspects:
- Accuracy｜准确性
- Freshness｜时效性
- Consistency｜一致性
- Completeness｜完整性
- Source Reliability｜来源可信

### 3. Legibility｜信息可理解性
Can AI clearly understand what the product is, its attributes, specifications, variants, and claims?

### 4. Matchability｜需求可匹配性
Can AI map the product to real consumer needs, use cases, constraints, and relevant scenarios?

### 5. Confidence｜决策信心
Does AI have enough relevant and reliable information to include and recommend the product with confidence?

## Role Split

**Foundational Conditions｜基础条件**
- Accessibility
- Information Quality

**Selection Core｜选择核心**
- Legibility
- Matchability
- Confidence

## Current Boundaries

- Single-product evaluation only｜单次只评估一个具体产品 / SKU
- Focus on product information rather than transaction/service operations
- Prioritize information that affects discovery, filtering and recommendation
- Do not optimize completeness at the expense of truthfulness
- Category-inherent variability should not automatically reduce information quality

## Key Principles

> **Human Trust ≠ AI Confidence**  
> 人的信任感，不完全等于 AI 的决策信心。

> **For this category, what information reduces the buyer’s uncertainty?**  
> 对于这个品类，什么信息能够减少购买决策中的不确定性？

> **Evidence should support the exact claim, not a broader interpretation.**  
> 证据只能支持它明确证明的那一层，不应被 AI 自行扩大。
